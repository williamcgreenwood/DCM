# Part A — What I like about DCM 6.0

The architectural direction is substantially stronger than the earlier prop-by-prop DCM. The important improvement is not model complexity; it is that the **sports world, market definition, platform settlement, and learning system are becoming separate contracts**. That separation is mathematically necessary.

|  # | Design object                                | What holds up                                                                                                                              | Failure it prevents                                                                                                     |
| -: | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1 | **EventWorld**                               | One sporting event is generated once and reused by every related market.                                                                   | Points/PRA, pass/rush combos, fantasy scores, etc. contradicting one another because they were simulated independently. |
|  2 | **PrimitiveStatLedger**                      | The ledger is the correct computational and truth boundary: primitives are stored once; derived markets are functions of those primitives. | Duplicate simulation, impossible composites, unnecessary CPU/memory use.                                                |
|  3 | **ConservationRule**                         | Structural identities are checked before market derivation, not repaired afterward.                                                        | Illegal worlds contaminating probabilities and correlations.                                                            |
|  4 | **OpportunityState vs EfficiencyState**      | Separating minutes/snaps/routes/targets/PA from per-opportunity performance is causally correct.                                           | Role changes masquerading as shooting/YPA/YPC “form,” or efficiency changes masquerading as workload.                   |
|  5 | **MarketDefinition**                         | Exact `(Platform, League, Market, DefinitionVersion)` boundaries are the only safe way to translate sports truth into prop truth.          | Fuzzy stat matching and silent unit/definition substitutions.                                                           |
|  6 | **Three-dimensional settlement**             | Administrative → Comparison → Economic is correct. DNP/Reboot/Tie cannot safely be collapsed into one `VOID` state.                        | Incorrect pick counts, same-team refund behavior, payout tiers, or learning labels.                                     |
|  7 | **EntryContract + payout display hash**      | Capturing the actual submitted payout is better than inferring economics from card size.                                                   | Applying a generic “6-pick pays X” table to a lineup whose displayed economics differed.                                |
|  8 | **`max(R_LB,R_MG)`**                         | Minimum Guarantee and Leaderboard are separate mechanisms; the customer receives the larger one, not their sum.                            | Inflated EV.                                                                                                            |
|  9 | **Fail-closed state**                        | `UNRESOLVED`, `UNKNOWN_PLATFORM_RULE`, `PRESEASON_RULE_UNVERIFIED`, etc. are legitimate outcomes.                                          | A guessed settlement silently turning into training data or money logic.                                                |
| 10 | **Offered-sides-only**                       | Analytical Higher/Lower can both exist, while selection remains constrained to the actual platform offer.                                  | Ranking a mathematically preferred side the user cannot actually select.                                                |
| 11 | **Green Goblin constructor veto**            | Making Goblin rejection structural is much stronger than a prompt instruction.                                                             | Goblins re-entering via debug mode, portfolio repair, ranking fallback, or empty-card pressure.                         |
| 12 | **Empty-card legality**                      | Six is a possible lineup size, not an optimization target.                                                                                 | Padding marginal or negative-utility plays to make output look complete.                                                |
| 13 | **Sport-plugin envelope**                    | Different sports can use different physical atoms while preserving the common EventWorld/Ledger/Settlement contracts.                      | A global schema accumulating `pitch_id`, `hole_id`, `rally_id`, etc. every time a new sport is added.                   |
| 14 | **Production/research authority separation** | Bartlett BEL, DPMM, GP-LVM and similar challengers do not receive production authority merely because they are sophisticated.              | Algorithm novelty becoming a substitute for unseen validation.                                                          |
| 15 | **Software version ≠ Learning Revision**     | `6.x` can advance engineering while remaining `LR000000`.                                                                                  | Engineering work being mislabeled as forecasting improvement.                                                           |
| 16 | **Hash lineage**                             | Evidence → parameters → worlds → ledger → market → projection → entry → settlement is the right provenance direction.                      | Reconstructing a forecast from mutable state after the fact.                                                            |
| 17 | **Tracking-data boundary**                   | NGS, Statcast, SportVU/Hawk-Eye-like data belong in evidence unless a versioned market definition explicitly maps them.                    | Vendor/tracking features becoming unofficial “primitive stats.”                                                         |

These are consistent with the frozen Master Blueprint and ADR: shared worlds, immutable primitives, explicit unknown state, exact platform definitions, Goblin exclusion and no forced card remain inherited law.  

The football slice also demonstrates that the abstraction is not purely theoretical. The reported WSAB implementation uses common NFL/CFB primitives, exact rush/dropback identities, receptions ≤ targets ≤ routes, derived combos from the same ledger, and one sport-agnostic `settle_world_lineup` path.  The associated report records **41/41 tests passing**, including corrupted-world rejection, Goblin veto, ties versus DNP/Reboot, unknown payout tables, and deterministic lineage. 

One qualification matters: those are **reported WSAB test results**, not an independent rerun from the full WSAB source tree in this review. The test report itself says it does not prove predictive improvement, live Reboot-list completeness, an official CFB eligibility list, real Leaderboard EV, or canonical v5.4.1 regression compatibility.

# Part B — What I do not like about DCM 6.0

## B1. Architectural debt / underspecification

| Issue                                               | Hazard                          | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No executable content-addressed DAG in WSAB**     | Correctness + cost + governance | v2 requires reusable nodes keyed by identity, cutoff, source versions, config, schema and parent hashes. WSAB still presents a conventional function tree.                                                                    |
| **No first-class node state machine**               | Correctness + interruption      | `PENDING → RUNNING → COMPLETE_VERIFIED / BLOCKED / INVALIDATED / FAILED_*` is specified but not demonstrated.                                                                                                                 |
| **No AdaptiveExecutionPlanner v2**                  | Cost + environment-fit          | Existing v5.4.1 `runtime_planner.py` estimates ETA/safe cutoff; that is not a planner selecting workers, batch sizes, spill mode or simulation tiers.                                                                         |
| **No ResourceGovernor v2**                          | Cost + environment-fit          | v5.4.1 already has request backpressure and bounded batching, but not total memory/cache/temp-disk/world-count/deadline governance.                                                                                           |
| **Rule semantics are still too branch-oriented**    | Correctness + cost              | Production wants compiled exact lookup by `Platform + EntryType + League + Market + RuleVersion + Situation`, with unknown keys failing closed.                                                                               |
| **No demonstrated sparse semantic portfolio graph** | Cost + correctness              | Same-event/shared-role/shared-weather edges should be explicit rather than dense board-wide pairwise work.                                                                                                                    |
| **Performance instrumentation is far short of v2**  | Governance                      | v5.4.1 has a useful timer and bounded LRU, but its stage metric only covers input/output rows, wall and CPU time. v2 requires RSS, Python allocation, I/O, cache, invalidation, tools, tokens, checkpoint and quality state.  |

The optimization blueprint is much stricter: a content-addressed DAG, descendant-only invalidation, five compute tiers, bounded state, online statistics, planner decisions, measured performance SLOs and explicit release blockers are part of the architecture—not optional polish. 

### Important correction to the earlier WSAB report

The WSAB report accurately said that **v5.4.1 was not present in that build environment**, so the football package was standalone forward-development rather than a verified canonical patch. 

That historical statement remains true. But **the canonical v5.4.1 files are available in the present project context now**. Its manifest declares:

* Source SHA-256: `bd1fb433d5f82d3812e453c30edcbb67db11b20f60e43cf50424c45a7c2ff474`
* Ledger SHA-256: `a9956ef1d231eb37ea5898b5145d660b986b68ee4dc6cfbd5c43fed59064c29a` 

So the next integration should **rebase the WSAB work onto those exact canonical bytes**, not continue treating v5.4.1 as unavailable.

The separate `PHASE_BC_SCHEMA_V1_2026-08-25` JSON bytes are still **UNVERIFIED in this review**. The documents declare SHA-256 `6e78dacc...`, but the original schema JSON itself has not been surfaced as an independently hashable attachment here.

## B2. Scope risk

The 15-plus-sport map is useful as an **architecture horizon** and dangerous as an implementation queue.

ADR-V6-001 is right to authorize football and E2E settlement, place MLB next, and defer AFL/CFL/LAX/handball/soccer/tennis/golf/cricket/UFC production registries. 

I would preserve that order:

**Basketball LIVE → football integration complete → MLB PA engine → then evidence-driven sport expansion.**

Trying to implement every sport now introduces three simultaneous unknowns: physical opportunity mechanics, conservation rules, and platform-administration rules. That is exactly where fabricated “generic sport logic” appears.

**Hazards:** correctness, cost, governance.

## B3. Known contract gaps

These need to be closed **before calling football platform settlement complete**:

| Gap                                               | Required behavior                                                                 | Hazard               |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| CFB fixture registry ≠ official qualifying roster | Freeze the dated official list and hash it.                                       | Correctness          |
| CFB event phase                                   | Require `game_phase == CFP_PLAYOFF` where that snapshot applies.                  | Correctness          |
| Generic `left_early`                              | Replace with explicit `left_first_half` + `no_second_half_return`.                | Correctness          |
| `board_id` / board scope                          | Distinguish full-game from 1H/1Q/season/preseason/etc.                            | Correctness          |
| Combo-square Reboot                               | Encode the exact OR/eligibility semantics, not a generic stat-key check.          | Correctness          |
| Same-player stack weight                          | Enforce the 0.95 Leaderboard override.                                            | Economic correctness |
| NFLP                                              | Keep physical modeling separate; settlement remains `UNRESOLVED` unless verified. | Correctness          |
| CFB regular season/bowls                          | Do not inherit CFP rules.                                                         | Correctness          |
| Leaderboard group distribution                    | Keep EV `UNMODELED` without actual group context.                                 | Governance           |
| Live Reboot stat list completeness                | Current tests cannot establish live-board completeness.                           | Correctness          |

The ADR itself identifies CFB/player-list ambiguity, NFL preseason failure closure and missing Leaderboard group information as unresolved data requirements. 

## B4. Optimization-contract gaps

This is the largest gap between **“DCM v6 architecture”** and **“optimized DCM v6 runtime.”**

There is currently no release evidence for:

1. real production E2E profiling;
2. cold/warm medians and worst-case dispersion;
3. PeakRSS tracking;
4. highest-allocation functions;
5. bottleneck before/after register;
6. largest-HAR bounded-memory proof;
7. 2× largest-board stress;
8. repeated-run leak test;
9. adaptive Monte Carlo convergence on a production board;
10. sequential/process/thread comparison;
11. sequential/parallel deterministic parity;
12. descendant-only invalidation measurements;
13. interruption at every major boundary followed by verified resume;
14. TokenBudgetManager metrics;
15. research-claim deduplication savings;
16. `ComputeROI` governance;
17. final quality/performance-frontier comparison;
18. all §22 performance artifacts.

Blueprint v2 explicitly says those are **release blockers**, not aspirational enhancements.  

## B5. Social/process risk

The largest governance risk is **document chronology drift**.

One document says “football is NEXT.” Another later document reports football as implemented. A future ChatGPT session may then read a sentence saying “accepted football layer” and silently convert that into “integrated, regression-tested canonical v6.”

Those are different states:

`DESIGNED → AUTHORIZED → IMPLEMENTED_STANDALONE → INTEGRATED → REGRESSION_VERIFIED → RELEASE_ACCEPTED`

DCM needs those statuses explicitly in machine-readable metadata.

Do not let natural-language phrases such as “accepted,” “done,” “live,” or “canonical-adjacent” serve as lifecycle state.

**Hazard:** governance first, correctness second.

# Part D — Things ChatGPT cannot honestly do

## D1. Plus Chat

Plus Chat cannot honestly:

* claim an E2E production runner was profiled because a prompt asked for optimization;
* guarantee byte-identical continuation after a dropped response when no checkpoint artifact was written;
* report PeakRSS of a program that ran somewhere else;
* infer a flame graph from source inspection;
* hold the largest HAR, all decoded responses, historical data, every EventWorld, line surfaces, workbook and release package in conversational context and call that bounded memory;
* promote `LR000000` because architecture tests pass;
* invent official PrizePicks Reboot rows for soccer, tennis, golf, CFL, etc.;
* reconstruct missing canonical source and then claim its expected SHA-256 was verified;
* select a Green Goblin “just once” to complete a card;
* make six selections the success criterion;
* turn session memory into a content-addressed cache;
* replace a deterministic probability kernel with prose reasoning.

## D2. ChatGPT Work

Work improves project-oriented execution, file handling and agentic web workflows. OpenAI currently documents persistent project context and cloud-browser tasks that can continue while the user is away. ([OpenAI Help Center][2])

It still cannot honestly be treated as:

* the user's laptop/VM profiler unless the production runner actually executes in an environment whose metrics are being measured;
* proof of a 2× largest-board memory SLO without actually running that workload;
* proof of bounded process-parallel behavior without the runner and parity tests;
* a guarantee of uninterrupted arbitrary compute for hours;
* authority for a PrizePicks rule absent from a verified source snapshot;
* authority to promote a Learning Revision;
* a replacement for canonical hash verification;
* a reason to bypass Goblin/offered-side/temporal gates;
* a reason to fabricate `Performance_Final.json`.

**Neither Chat nor Work may turn “the architecture says this should be fast” into “this was measured to be fast.”**

# Part J — Risks if you try to build all of DCM 6.0 inside ChatGPT Plus

The likely failure mode is not one dramatic mathematical error. It is **state decay across a long workflow**.

A large HAR is processed. Research begins. The response or runtime ends after half the events. No atomic `PrimitiveLedgerNode` checkpoint exists. The next conversation reconstructs progress from prose. It cannot prove which player evidence was already incorporated, so several events are researched again. One status source has updated in the meantime, producing a temporal mismatch.

Then two props from the same event get independently reconstructed distributions because the shared EventWorld artifact was lost. Their opportunity assumptions diverge.

Context pressure increases. Instead of storing the whole board in a structured artifact, low-priority PASS rows disappear from the response. Full-board accounting is no longer provable.

A CFB player leaves early. Generic football logic sees `left_early=True` and applies NFL-like Reboot behavior because `game_phase`, official qualifying-player identity, `left_first_half`, and `no_second_half_return` were not captured.

A new baseball feature needs PA state. Someone creates a global `PlateAppearance` common-schema object because that is convenient in the current prompt, bypassing the frozen sport-envelope rule.

The card has only four strong plays. Two PASS rows are promoted because “we need six.”

The architecture compiles and tests. Someone names it `LR000001`.

Finally, `Performance_Final.json` is written with numbers inferred from one sandbox timing and the release is described as optimized.

At that point practically every important v6 boundary has been violated even though each individual shortcut looked small.

That is precisely why the Optimization Blueprint makes artifacts, checkpoints, profiling and release gates structural requirements rather than prompt suggestions.
