# Universal sport research expansion

Status: implemented research/control-plane foundation; no production-model promotion.

This increment operationalizes the sport-family requirements supplied in the
Universal Adaptive Research OS. It does not claim that a generic model can
replace native sport physics.

## Executable coverage

The canonical catalog now covers gridiron, basketball, baseball, soccer,
hockey, cricket, combat/boxing, racket sports, golf, motorsport, Australian
rules, handball, lacrosse, darts, rugby, volleyball, and esports.

For every family the runtime has:

- deterministic aliases and a canonical sport ID;
- event and segment structure;
- native subject type and opportunity resources;
- world-kind and high-yield co-extraction strategy;
- environment applicability;
- native participation, opportunity, efficiency, affiliation, counterparty,
  event, availability, normalization, freshness and advanced research fields;
- exact source-catalog candidates in the host research plan;
- an import-validated 24-component SportPlugin completion ledger.

`SubjectOfferSet` identity is namespaced by sport and competition so provider-
local subject/event IDs cannot merge across sports.

## Runtime states

`PRODUCTION_SUPPORTED` and `SHADOW_SUPPORTED` keep their existing exact
league/market gates. A known `RESEARCH_ONLY` family can be accounted,
grouped, indexed, archived and researched only when research-shadow is
explicitly enabled. It remains `UNSUPPORTED` for modeling/selection until its
native EventWorld, primitive/conservation rules, MarketDefinitions,
distributions, settlement and validation components are implemented.

Unknown sports still fail closed. Missing or unavailable Higher/Lower sides,
Green Goblins, permanent subject exclusions, live offers, unknown modifiers,
and unresolved identity continue to block research or selection according to
the canonical funnel.

## Outlier priority

Outlier is now a first-class captured offer/MarketDefinition source in the
versioned SourceCatalog. It has lower exact retrieval cost than PrizePicks but
is never treated as an official player-performance or settlement authority.
The target-book offer, exact side availability, line, odds, modifier, ORF and
hit-rate fields are retained as market evidence; independent player and
matchup research remains mandatory before any recommendation can be eligible.

## Learning boundary

The universal ML registry, feature families and calibration policy are visible
as partial components for research-only sports. They are not activated as
trained champions. `LR000000` and predictive claim `NONE` remain unchanged
until chronological, unseen settlements earn promotion.
