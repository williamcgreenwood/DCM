# Universal Algorithmic Computation and Research Discovery Law

**Version:** `PILLARS-UNIVERSAL-ALGORITHMIC-COMPUTATION-v1.0.0-20261014`
**Status:** REQUIRED_CORE / PERMANENT / INHERITED
**Scope:** Every DCM run, all seven advanced-modeling capabilities, and future sport adapters.

This law extends the Algorithmic Constitution and Consumption Law. It does not create a second model, research store, or execution path. Canonical implementations are under `src/dcm`; research evidence and method-discovery records use the existing Research OS and `ResearchStore`. The seven mathematical capabilities remain distinct from the infrastructure that makes them reproducible.

## Required computation procedures

Each capability must use or explicitly abstain from the following procedures according to input size, data shape, rights, and measured resource cost:

| Procedure | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Search | Exact canonical IDs and composite keys first; then temporal and content-addressed lookup, structured indexes, lexical ranking, graph traversal, and bounded fuzzy/semantic candidate discovery. Approximate retrieval proposes candidates; verified evidence establishes identity. |
| Sort and rank | Deterministic stable ordering for final output; partial Top-K before expensive ranking; heap/selection methods only when their measured workload fits; report rank stability where it affects a decision. |
| Group | Canonical grouping by event, subject, role epoch, market, and temporal cohort before shared computation. Never merge identities or time regimes on similarity alone. |
| Index | Reuse exact/composite, temporal, content-hash, and structured indexes. Add or change an index only with a consumer and a measured access pattern. |
| Append | Preserve immutable forecast, evidence, model, execution, audit, and settlement history. Corrections reference the original record; retries are idempotent; transactional or append-only persistence retains restart semantics. |
| Archive | Version schemas, algorithms, parameters, artifacts, and retention decisions. Publish sanitized artifacts only through existing validated storage routes, with readback verification before calling them durable. |
| Hash | Use canonical SHA-256 content hashes for material inputs, transforms, model/config versions, outputs, receipts, and archives. Record the algorithm/version and hashes in lineage; a hash proves byte identity, not source truth or model validity. |
| Learn | Use appropriate baselines, chronological/event-grouped evaluation, calibration, ablation, drift checks, champion/challenger governance, and rollback. Training or fitted parameters alone do not prove predictive validity. |

Every algorithm record and runtime receipt identifies applicability, algorithm/version, inputs and cutoff, parameters, producer, consumer, fallback or blocker, output hash, diagnostics, tests, and resource evidence where applicable. Do not force a procedure that is statistically inappropriate or unsupported by data. Every capability is machine-learning-ready through a governed path, but may abstain from training until data, rights, and validation requirements are met.

## Rights-aware research intelligence

External papers, datasets, code, repositories, news, market information, and community posts are discovery leads until individually reviewed. A discovery record must preserve source identity/hash, rights and license state, hypothesis, expected benefit, possible harm, required data, experiment and metric evidence, tests, and downstream consumer. Source availability does not imply permission to retain content, train on it, or execute its code.

Never bypass access controls, execute downloaded code automatically, or train on content whose rights do not authorize that use. Publicly visible community content is not automatically licensed for model training. Independently verify factual claims; social sentiment cannot override verified evidence. Keep full copyrighted pages and restricted response bodies out of the repository and model datasets unless explicit rights permit that use. Record `DISCOVERY_ONLY`, `RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, or a more restrictive state until specific permission is evidenced.

Promotion requires a traceable experiment, permitted inputs, tests, a real runtime consumer, and predeclared process or chronological predictive evidence appropriate to the claim. Retrospective fit, synthetic fixtures, citations, registry entries, hashes, and attractive community performance claims are not promotion evidence.

## Seven-capability inheritance and certification

KDE, SVD, shared EventWorld Monte Carlo, the algorithm execution graph, calibrated probabilities, portfolio dependence, and payout-specific EV inherit every applicable requirement above. Capability-specific requirements and producer/consumer connections remain in the advanced modeling execution contract. KDE/SVD diagnostics cannot manufacture forecast probabilities; portfolio joint outcomes must use aligned shared worlds; EV remains blocked without calibrated joint outcomes and verified payout rules.

The canonical execution manifest binds this law's version and SHA-256. A missing law identity is an execution failure. Preserve `LR000000`, predictive claim `NONE`, and existing production certification gates until independent chronological evidence earns a change.
